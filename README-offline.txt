SCAT 2e offline course - how to use
====================================
1. Unzip the whole folder (keep the folders together).
2. Double-click index.html. That is the course index: all 16 chapters, every page,
   flash cards, practice tests and exams, plus search and a "continue where you left off" button.
3. Every course page has a round/pill "Index" button, an Index item in the top menu,
   and the Left/Right arrow keys move to the previous/next page.

What works offline
- Flash cards (real glossary data), with "I know this" tracking.
- Practice tests, chapter exams, Check Your Understanding and checkpoints:
  generated from each chapter's glossary terms and chapter-summary sentences.
  These are NOT the original online questions (those live on the school's server and
  were not in the export). Scores are saved in this browser only.
- quiz.html: build a mixed practice quiz or timed exam from any chapters.
- Short-answer worksheets save in this browser (they are not sent to an instructor).

Not in the export (cannot be restored offline)
- Images (the /media folder was not included) and the Competency Checklist PDFs.
