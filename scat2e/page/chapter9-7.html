Part 2: Training

9.7 Procedural Lab

Safety Alert

Avoiding air turbulence in the buffer room is important to maintaining a controlled environment for sterile compounding. Therefore, personnel should gather their supplies before entering the buffer room.

This lab walks you through the step-by-step actions that you must follow to clean and disinfect the horizontal LAFW. Take your time. Work through each step methodically and with close attention to detail.

 

Note: For the purposes of this procedural lab, you will not perform hand-washing procedures unless directed to do so by your instructor. Should your instructor ask you to perform these procedures, refer to Chapter 8 for instructions. In practice, hand hygiene is mandatory. In that instance, you would also need supplies related to hand hygiene including soap in a nonrefillable dispenser and disposable nail cleaners, also referred to as nail picks.

 

 

Essential Supplies

To complete the Unit 2 procedural labs, you will need to ensure that various essential anteroom and buffer room supplies such as those listed in Table 9.2 are available for your use. If you are using a PEC other than a LAFW, you will need to alter some procedures to account for the type of PEC and the direction of airflow coming from the HEPA filter. In this case, refer to instructor direction or facility SOPs for specific instructions regarding this procedural lab.

 

TABLE 9.2 Essential Anteroom and Buffer Room Supplies for Sterile Compounding
Anteroom Supplies
  • pen
  • garbing supplies, including disposable, low-lint shoe covers; disposable, low-lint head and facial hair covers (if needed); disposable face mask; goggles for eye protection (if needed); low-lint gown; and sterile, powder-free, disposable gloves
  • low-lint wipers
  • alcohol-based hand rub
  • sterile 70% IPA
  • waste container
  • table or transport cart
  • PEC cleaning and disinfection checklist (may also be kept in buffer room or outside cleanroom suite)

Note: As mentioned earlier, the Chapter 9 Procedural Lab does not include steps for performing hand-washing procedures. If your instructor directs you to perform handwashing as part of the Chapter 9 Procedural Lab, you will need access to a sink and hand-washing supplies. Refer to Chapter 8 for details on hand hygiene procedures. Remember that, in practice, you must perform hand hygiene procedures prior to preparing a CSP in a sterile compounding area.
Buffer Room Supplies
  • pen and a permanent marker
  • sterile water
  • EPA-registered, one-step disinfectant cleaner
  • sterile 70% IPA in a pour bottle
  • sterile, low-lint wipers
  • waste container
  • table or transport cart
  • open-sided, horizontal LAFW

Note: For the Chapter 9 Procedural Lab, you will not wipe the interior surfaces of the PEC with sterile water to remove debris or residue. Follow instructor direction or facility SOPs regarding which situations require you to wipe the interior surfaces of the PEC with sterile water. If you are instructed to wipe with sterile water, wipe surfaces in the same order and using the same stroke pattern as what is identified in Figures 9.3 and 9.4.

 

 

Procedure

Practice Tip

Certain garbing and hood cleaning procedures may vary based on the location and type of PEC (LAFW, RABS, BSC), and the CSP category being prepared (Category 1, 2, 3, HD CSPs). To complete the Chapter 9 procedural lab using a PEC other than an open-sided LAFW, refer to facility SOPs for specific instructions for cleaning and disinfecting the PEC.

Take a careful, deliberate approach to each detailed step of this procedural lab. In practice, using this approach helps to ensure an appropriate environment for sterile compounding. Refer to instructor directions or facility SOPs to determine whether goggles are required for the procedure and whether sterile gloves should be donned in the anteroom or buffer room.

 

Note: For steps 1–2, please refer to Chapter 5 for gathering and staging supplies. For steps 3–11, refer to Chapter 8 for a reminder of the step-by step procedures that are required for garbing. Steps 1–11 and step 32 should be performed in the anteroom. Steps 12–30 should be performed in the buffer room. Step 31 may be performed in the anteroom, buffer room, or outside of the cleanroom suite.

 

 

Gathering and Staging Supplies

Best Practice

Because sterile 70% IPA is a supply item used in both the anteroom and the buffer room, it is best practice to store this item in both areas.

  1. Prior to entering the buffer room, you must gather all of the supplies that you need for the cleaning and disinfection of the horizontal LAFW: one bottle of sterile water; one bottle of EPA-registered, one-step disinfectant cleaner; one bottle of sterile 70% IPA; sterile, low-lint wipers in their original package; and a permanent marker. To stage these supply items, don gloves and then use low-lint wipers to wipe down the exterior surfaces of all supplies with a sporicidal agent, EPA-registered disinfectant, or sterile 70% IPA before moving them to the clean side of the anteroom.
  2. Place the supply items on a clean table or transport cart, or in a bin on the clean side of the anteroom. (These work surfaces outside of a PEC must be cleaned and disinfected each day of use.)

 

 

Garbing

Practice Tip

If using a face mask with ear loops, personnel may want to either don the face mask prior to donning the head cover or lift up the edge of the head cover, loop the face mask directly around the ears, and then pull the head cover down over the ears.

  1. Don shoe covers while stepping over the line of demarcation into the clean side of the anteroom.
  2. Don a head cover.
  3. Don a face mask.
  4. Don a facial hair cover (if needed).
  5. Don goggles for eye protection (if needed).

    Note: For the purpose of this training lab, you will skip handwashing, unless told otherwise by your instructor.

  6. Don a gown.
  7. Apply an alcohol-based hand rub to all surfaces of your hands and allow your hands to dry thoroughly.
  8. Don sterile gloves.
  9. Apply sterile 70% IPA to all surfaces of your gloves and allow your gloved hands to dry thoroughly.
USP <797>

USP <797> requires that sterile compounding personnel use an alcohol-based hand rub on all surfaces of the hands and allow these surfaces to dry before donning sterile gloves.

 

 

Arranging Supplies

  1. Move the supply items into the buffer room and place the items on a clean table or cart near the horizontal LAFW.
  2. Remove the outer wrapper from the sterile, low-lint wipers and place the package in the outer six-inch zone of the PEC. Discard the wrapper into the waste container.
  3. Remove the outer wrapper from the sterile 70% IPA and place the bottle in the outer six-inch zone of the PEC. Discard the outer wrapper into the waste container.

 

 

Cleaning the Horizontal LAFW

Safety Alert

Sterile compounding personnel should never let fluid come into contact with the PEC’s HEPA filter. This action may cause damage to the filter.

Safety Alert

To maintain sterility, individually wrapped, sterile, low-lint wipers must be removed from the package within the DCA of the hood. Sterile, low-lint wipers that are packaged with multiple wipers per package should be opened, the wipers removed, and then the package resealed all within the DCA. Sterile 70% IPA and sterile water bottles should be opened to saturate wipers and then reclosed within the DCA.

Practice Tip

In this lab, you will only wipe the work surface of the PEC to remove debris and residue. In practice, you may need to wipe down all interior surfaces of the PEC to remove debris or visible residue. If so, follow the same procedures shown in steps 22–29 using sterile water.

Practice Tip

Refer to facility SOPs to determine whether used wipers should be placed directly into the waste container, or instead should be placed into a discard pile in the outer 6” of the hood. In that case, once all six wipers had been used, they would then place all six used wipers into the waste container at the end of the procedure, and then would apply sterile 70% IPA to the surfaces of the gloves and allow them to dry, prior to commencing with the next steps in the procedure.

Practice Tip

Personnel must remember to use a down-and-up motion when cleaning the side panels of a horizontal LAFW and a side-to-side motion when cleaning the HEPA filter screen, ceiling, and work surface of a horizontal LAFW. They should ensure that each stroke overlaps the previous stroke slightly as they move from the innermost part of the PEC to the outer edge.

  1. Apply sterile 70% IPA to your sterile gloves and rub your hands together to wet the entire surface of each glove. Allow your gloves to dry naturally.
  2. Move the package of sterile, low-lint wipers from the outer six-inch zone to the DCA. Open the package, remove one wiper, and place it in the DCA. Then close the package and return it to the nearby table or cart.
  3. Move the sterile 70% IPA to the DCA. Open the bottle and pour a small amount of liquid onto the wiper. The amount of liquid should be enough to saturate the wiper but not so much that it pools on the PEC’s work surface. When the wiper is saturated, recap the bottle, label the bottle with the BUD, and place it on a nearby table or cart.
  4. Pick up the wiper and wipe the exterior surface of the sterile water bottle. Then place the sterile water bottle in the DCA and discard the wiper into the waste container.
  5. Open the package of sterile, low-lint wipers, remove one wiper, and place it in the DCA. Then close the package and return it to the nearby table or cart.
  6. Open the bottle of sterile water and pour a small amount of liquid onto the wiper. The amount of liquid should be enough to saturate the wiper but not so much that it pools on the PEC’s work surface. When the wiper is saturated, recap the bottle, label it with the BUD, and place it on a nearby table or cart.
  7. Pick up the wiper and, beginning at the interior corner of the work surface, wipe the work surface of the PEC using overlapping horizontal strokes, moving in side-to-side strokes from the area closest to the HEPA filter out to the front edge of the hood, removing any visible residue that is present on the work surface of the PEC. Discard the wiper into the waste container.
  8. Open the package of sterile, low-lint wipers, remove six additional wipers, and place the stack of wipers in the DCA. Then close the package and place the package on a nearby table or cart.
  9. Open the bottle of one-step disinfectant cleaner and pour a small amount of liquid onto the stack of wipers located within the DCA. The amount of liquid should be enough to saturate the wipers but not so much that it pools on the PEC’s work surface. The wipers should be wet enough to leave a streak when wiped on the PEC’s work surface, but not so wet as to leave pools of water, drip or run down the sides of the PEC, or leak into the HEPA filter. When the wipers are saturated, recap the bottle, label it with the BUD, and place it on a nearby table or cart.
  10. Select the first wiper from the top of the stack. Carefully clean the screen covering of the HEPA filter, being careful not to let liquid enter through the openings of the screen. Starting at a top corner of the filter screen, use overlapping strokes to move from side to side and then from top to bottom. When the entire surface has been cleaned, place the used wiper into the waste container.
  11. Select a second, clean wiper from the top of the stack and clean the ceiling of the horizontal LAFW. Starting at a back corner of the PEC’s ceiling, use overlapping, side-to-side strokes and move toward the outer edge of the PEC. When you reach the outside edge, wipe down the front edge of the PEC’s ceiling and then place the used wiper into the waste container.
  12. Select a third, clean wiper from the top of the stack and clean the hang bar and hooks of the horizontal LAFW. When finished, place the used wiper into the waste container.
  13. Select a fourth, clean wiper from the top of the stack and clean the side panels of the horizontal LAFW. First, clean one side of the PEC starting at the back upper corner. Use overlapping down-and-up strokes and move from the interior of the PEC to the outside edge. When you reach the outside edge of the PEC’s side panel, wipe down the front edge of the side and then place the used wiper into the waste container.

  14. Select a fifth, clean wiper from the top of the stack and clean the opposite side panel of the horizontal LAFW in a similar manner. Then place the used wiper into the waste container.
  15. Select a sixth, clean wiper from the top of the stack. Clean the work surface of the horizontal LAFW by using overlapping, side-to-side strokes. Start at a back corner of the PEC and move toward the outer edge of the PEC. When you reach the outer edge, wipe down the front edge of the PEC’s work surface, then place the used wiper into the waste container.

  16. Repeat steps 22–29 using sterile 70% IPA in place of the one-step disinfectant cleaner.

 

 

Completing the PEC Cleaning and Disinfection Checklist and Removing Garb

Practice Tip

Recording initials and BUD may take place in the anteroom or buffer room. Refer to facility SOPs for specific instructions.

  1. Record the date, time, and your signature or initials on the PEC cleaning and disinfection checklist.
  2. Return the transport cart or bin and any applicable supply items to the anteroom.
  3. In the anteroom, remove and dispose of garb according to Chapter 8 and your facility’s SOPs.
