Part 2: Training

4.7 Examine the Resources and Supplies

Sterile compounding personnel must be familiar with USP <797> standards that address the cleaning, disinfection, and general maintenance of the anteroom, buffer room, and PECs. A close examination of the resources and supplies associated with these procedures provides personnel with the understanding they need to maintain quality assurance in the sterile compounding environment.

 

 

USP–NF General Chapter <1072> Disinfectants and Antiseptics

As discussed in previous chapters of this textbook, USP <797> plays a critical role in determining the layout and design of the sterile compounding environment, as well as the creation of policies and procedures related to all aspects of sterile compounding and aseptic technique. USP <797> also mandates the products, procedures, and schedules for the cleaning and disinfection of surfaces in the cleanroom suite. These surfaces are potential sources of contamination and include structural design fixtures in the anteroom and buffer room as well as the PECs themselves.

 

One chapter within the USP–NF, called General Chapter <1072> Disinfectants and Antiseptics, specifically addresses the classification, microbial activity, and appropriate selection of cleaning and disinfectant solutions. For that reason, sterile compounding personnel should have a good understanding of the content in this chapter as well.

 

 

Anteroom and Buffer Room Cleaning and Disinfection Checklists

Practice Tip

For an SCA, sterile compounding personnel record the cleaning and disinfection procedures. Personnel should refer to their facility’s SOPs manual for specific instructions.

Sterile compounding personnel must record the daily and monthly cleaning and disinfection procedures, as well as recording the application of a sporicidal agent for the anteroom on anteroom cleaning and disinfection checklists (see Figure 4.6). Personnel must record the same information about the buffer room cleaning and disinfection procedures on separate buffer room checklists (see Figure 4.7). Some facilities may choose to use one checklist for the cleaning and disinfection of both the anteroom and buffer room.
