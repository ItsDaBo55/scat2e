PART 2: TRAINING

1.9 Exploratory Lab

Now that you, as a sterile compounding student, have a basic understanding of sterile compounding facilities and personnel, you have the opportunity to explore important resources that set standards for the education and training of sterile compounding pharmacists and pharmacy technicians and track their continuing education requirements. The purpose of this exploratory lab is to provide you with hands-on experience in examining these resources: ASHP/ACPE Accreditation Standards for Pharmacy Education and Training Programs; ASHP’s Model Curriculum for Pharmacy Education and Training Programs, Fifth Edition (2019); and CPE Monitor, the continuing education online repository of the National Association of Boards of Pharmacy (NABP), which is where ACPE-accredited continuing education units (CEUs) are uploaded once completed.

 

Note: Some of the steps ask you to read and respond to one or more multiple-choice questions (designated in orange type, such as 1–1 and 1–2). Record your answers to these multiple-choice questions by either circling the correct answers on the pages or recording and submitting your answers as directed by your instructor.

Procedure

Take a careful, deliberate approach to each detailed step of this exploratory lab. In practice, using this approach ensures your understanding of the resources necessary for real-world sterile compounding.

 

 

Understanding the ASHP/ACPE Accreditation Standards for Pharmacy Technician Education and Training Programs

  1. Open the link to the ASHP/ACPE Accreditation Standards.
  2. Review the document’s Table of Contents.
  3. Answer the following multiple-choice question, recording your answer as directed by your instructor.

    3–1. Which section of the ASHP/ACPE Accreditation Standards addresses the qualifications of program faculty?

    1. Section I
    2. Section II
    3. Section III
    4. Section IV
  4. Go to Section I of the ASHP/ACPE Accreditation Standards.
  5. Review Standard 3: Processing and Handling of Medications and Medication Orders.
  6. Answer the following multiple-choice question, recording your answer as directed by your instructor.

    6–1. Which competency is not listed as an entry-level competency?

    1. Assist pharmacists in preparing, storing, and distributing medication products including those requiring special handling and documentation.
    2. Explain procedures and communication channels to use in the event of a product recall or shortage, a medication error, or identification of another problem.
    3. Describe basic concepts related to preparation for sterile and nonsterile compounding.
    4. Prepare compounded sterile preparations per applicable, current USP Chapters.
  7. Advance to Standard 5: Regulatory and Compliance Knowledge and Skills.
  8. Answer the following multiple-choice question, recording your answer as directed by your instructor.

    8–1. Which competency is not listed as an entry-level competency?

    1. Describe the process and responsibilities required to obtain and maintain registration and/or licensure to work as a pharmacy technician.
    2. Describe pharmacy compliance with professional standards and relevant legal, regulatory, formulary, contractual, and safety requirements.
    3. Describe OSHA requirements for prevention and response to blood-borne pathogen exposure (e.g., accidental needlestick, postexposure prophylaxis).
    4. Describe major trends, issues, goals, and initiatives taking place in the pharmacy profession.
  9. Go to Standard 8: Advisory Committee.
  10. Answer the following multiple-choice question, recording your answer as directed by your instructor.

    10–1. Which area does not fall under the specific authority of a program’s advisory committee?

    1. faculty
    2. curriculum
    3. experiential training sites
    4. criteria for admission and dismissal

 

 

Understanding the ASHP’s Model Curriculum for Pharmacy Technician Education and Training Programs, Fifth Edition (2019)

  1. Open the link to ASHP’s Model Curriculum.
  2. Review the Overview that lists the standard categories and corresponding goals.
  3. Answer the following multiple-choice questions, recording your answers as directed by your instructor.

    13–1. Of the 15 standard categories addressed by the ASHP/ACPE Accreditation Standards for Pharmacy Technician Education and Training Programs, how many of these standard categories are the sole focus of ASHP’s Model Curriculum?

    1. 3 standard categories
    2. 4 standard categories
    3. 5 standard categories
    4. 6 standard categories

    13–2. Which standard category is one category that is not covered in ASHP’s Model Curriculum?

    1. Assessment of Competency Expectations
    2. Personal/Interpersonal Knowledge and Skills
    3. Patient Care, Quality and Safety Knowledge and Skills
    4. Processing and Handling of Medications and Medication Orders
  4. Review the grid that organizes the standards of ASHP’s Model Curriculum into the following columns: Entry-Level or Advanced-Level: Key Element; Learning Modality: Didactic; Learning Modality: Simulated (Lab); and Learning Modality (Experiential).
  5. Answer the following multiple-choice questions, recording your answers as directed by your instructor.

    15–1. According to ASHP’s Model Curriculum, which key element addresses the preparation of CSPs per applicable, current USP Chapters?

    1. standard 1.4
    2. standard 2.9
    3. standard 3.16
    4. standard 3.23

    15–2. Which student activity would be listed as a didactic activity?

    1. participate in a role-playing scenario
    2. engage in class discussion
    3. compound sterile preparations
    4. assist in a pharmacy wellness fair

 

 

Exploring Continuing Education Requirements for Pharmacists and Pharmacy Technicians

  1. Open the Medscape link that lists the continuing education requirements for pharmacists.
  2. Click on your state to review the continuing education requirements for pharmacists in your state.
  3. Answer the following short-answer questions, recording your answers as directed by your instructor.

    18–1. How many continuing education hours does your state board of pharmacy require for pharmacists?

    18–2. How often must the continuing hours be completed?

    18–3. Does the state board of pharmacy require the hours to be from an ACPE-accredited program? If so, how many of the hours must be ACPE-accredited?

    18–4. Does the state board of pharmacy require that any of the hours be related to a particular topic? If so, what topic(s)?

  4. Open the link that lists the continuing education requirements for pharmacy technicians.
  5. Click on your state to review the continuing education requirements for pharmacy technicians in your state.
  6. Answer the following short-answer questions, recording your answers as directed by your instructor.

    21–1. How many continuing education hours does your state board of pharmacy require for pharmacy technicians?

    21–2. How often must the continuing hours be completed?

    21–3. Does the state board of pharmacy require the hours to be from an ACPE-accredited program? If so, how many of the hours must be ACPE-accredited?

    21–4. Does the state board of pharmacy require that any of the hours be related to a particular topic? If so, what topic(s)?

 

 

Exploring the CPE Monitor

  1. Open the link to the CPE Monitor repository on NABP’s website.
  2. Scroll down to the section titled How CPE Monitor Works. Under that section, review the five-point CPE Monitor System Data Flow, and note the bulleted guidelines for submitting continuing education activities to CPE Monitor.
  3. If you have not already created your NABP account, skip to Step 25. If you already have an NABP e-Profile ID (ePID) number, scroll to the top of the page and click on the tab NABP e-Profile Login. Log in to your NABP account; then answer the following short-answer questions, recording your answers as directed by your instructor.

    24–1. How many continuing education hours are listed in your account as completed with the past 24 months?

    24–2. How many more continuing education hours must you complete to satisfy your state board of pharmacy requirement?

  4. If you have not already created your NABP account, scroll to the top of the page and click on the tab NABP e-Profile Login. Create your NABP account; then answer the following short-answer question, recording your answer as directed by your instructor.

    25–1. What is your NABP e-Profile ID (ePID) number?

    Be sure to record this number, for you will need to enter it to access your e-Profile throughout your pharmacy career.
