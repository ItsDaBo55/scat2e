Part 2: Training

2.5 Exploratory Lab

Now that you, as a sterile compounding student, have an understanding of medication safety—including risk-reduction strategies for medication errors, error-reporting programs, and helpful resources—you have the opportunity to visualize and explore some highly regarded resources used by pharmacy personnel. These resources include To Err is Human: Building a Safer Health System, the ISMP Guidelines for the Safe Preparation of Compounded Sterile Preparations, and the ISMP’s Medication Errors Reporting Program (ISMP MERP).

 

Note: Some of the steps ask you to read and respond to one or more multiple-choice questions (designated in orange type, such as 1–1 and 1–2). Record your answers to these multiple-choice questions by either circling the correct answers on the pages or recording and submitting your answers as directed by your instructor.

 

 

Supplies

  • To Err is Human: Building a Safer Health System (print or electronic version)
  • ISMP Guidelines for the Safe Preparation of Compounded Sterile Preparations (print or electronic 2016 revision)
  • ISMP’s Medication Errors Reporting Program (electronic program)

 

 

Procedure

Take a careful, deliberate approach to each detailed step of this exploratory lab. In practice, using this approach ensures your understanding of the skills necessary for real-world sterile compounding.

 

 

Examining To Err is Human: Building a Safer Health System

  1. Go the following website of the National Academies of Sciences, Engineering, and Medicine: https://SterileComp2.ParadigmEducation.com/NAPHumanErr. Download a free PDF of To Err is Human: Building a Safer Health System.

  2. Go to the Chapter 2 section titled “Introduction” on page 27.

    Courtesy of the National Academies Press/National Academies of Sciences, Engineering, and Medicine. Used with permission.

     

  3. Read the “Introduction” section in its entirety.

  4. Answer the following multiple-choice question, recording your answer as directed by your instructor.
    4–1.    According to the “Introduction” section, what term refers to medication errors that do not result in harm?

    1. medication misadventures

    2. medication events

    3. adverse events

    4. near misses

  5. Continue to the Chapter 2 section titled “How Frequently Do Errors Occur?” on page 29.

  6. Read the “How Frequently Do Errors Occur?” section in its entirety.

  7. Answer the following multiple-choice question, recording your answer as directed by your instructor.
    7–1.    According to the “How Frequently Do Errors Occur?” section, which statement is not true regarding studies related to adverse events?

    1. An adverse event is frequently caused by a patient’s underlying illness or condition.

    2. Many adverse events are the result of errors.

    3. Drug complications are the most common type of adverse event.
    4. Most adverse events occur in hospitals.
  8. Go to the Chapter 2 section titled “Factors That Contribute to Errors” on page 35.
  9. Read the “Studies of Adverse Events” section and the “Studies of Medication Errors” section in their entirety.
  10. Answer the following multiple-choice question, recording your answer as directed by your instructor.
    10–1.    According to the “Studies of Adverse Events” section and the “Studies of Medication Errors” section, which statement is not true regarding adverse events?

    1. An example of an adverse event is a treatment-related infection.

    2. More than two-thirds of adverse events are preventable.

    3. Most adverse events are voluntarily reported.

    4. The most common types of adverse events are those events associated with knowledge or the application of knowledge.

  11. Go to Table 8–1 on pages 184–189 of the Chapter 8 section titled “Selected Strategies to Improve Medication Safety.”

  12. Read Table 8–1 in its entirety.

  13. Answer the following multiple-choice question, recording your answer as directed by your instructor.
    13–1.  According to Table 8–1 of the “Selected Strategies to Improve Medication Safety” section, which statement is true regarding medication safety recommendations?

    1. Healthcare personnel adhere to one list of medication safety recommendations.

    2. Patients play an important role in medication safety and should be educated regarding their health care.

    3. The use of technology leads to increased adverse events and should be avoided whenever possible.

    4. Abbreviations are important time-savers in health care and should be used whenever possible.

  14. Close the publication To Err is Human: Building a Safer Health System.

 

 

Understanding the ISMP Guidelines for Safe Preparation of Compounded Sterile Preparations

  1. Go to the following website of the Institute for Safe Medication Practices: https://SterileComp2.ParadigmEducation.com/ISMPGuidelines. Access a PDF of the ISMP Guidelines for Safe Preparation of Compounded Sterile Preparations.

    © 2016 ISMP, all rights reserved. Visit www.ismp.org for more medication safety information, or to report medication errors or near misses to the ISMP Medication Errors Reporting Program (MERP)./span>

  2. Read the section titled “Background on Sterile Preparation Compounding Safety.”

  3. Answer the following multiple-choice questions, recording your answers as directed by your instructor.

17–1.  According to the “Background on Sterile Preparation Compounding Safety” section, what was the ratio of compounded sterile preparations that were prepared incorrectly prior to dispensing?

a. 1:5

b. 1:10

c. 1:25

d. 1:50


17–2.  According to the “Background on Sterile Preparation Compounding Safety” section, which type of CSP had reported errors as high as 37%, as revealed in this ISMP study?

a. cardioplegia solution

b. dialysis solution

c. parenteral nutrition (PN) solution

d. chemotherapy solution

  1. Read the remainder of the publication.

  2. Answer the following multiple-choice questions, recording your answers as directed by your instructor.

19–1.  According to the publication, which action should be prohibited?

a. syringe pull-back method

b. independent double-check

c. use of commercially manufactured products

d. use of customized order-entry templates


19–2.  According to the publication, which term is an alert that indicates something is outside of a predetermined safe range and, consequently, cannot be overridden by a staff member?

a. customized order-entry template

b. soft limit

c. process change control

d. hard limit

 

 

Using the ISMP’s Medication Errors Reporting Program

  1. Go to the ISMP’s website for the reporting of medication errors: https://SterileComp2.ParadigmEducation.com/ErrorReportingPrograms.
  2. Read the “Error Reporting and Analysis” section in its entirety.
  3. Answer the following multiple-choice question, recording your answer as directed by your instructor.


22–1.  Which description does not apply to the ISMP’s website?

a. free reporting of a medication error

b. voluntary submission of a medication error

c. error insights that are not shared with healthcare organizations

d. separate programs for healthcare practitioners and consumers to report a medication error

  1. Click on the link ISMP National Medication Errors Reporting Program (ISMP MERP).

  2. Answer the following multiple-choice question, recording your answer as directed by your instructor.


24–1.  Which item is not requested by the ISMP on the medication error reporting form?

a. identified prescription order information

b. name, dosage form, and strength of medication

c. contributing factors to medication error

d. actual or potential outcome of patient involved
